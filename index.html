Privacy Policy (English)

This Privacy Policy applies to the iPhone app “Color Drop” (the “App”), developed by PLHT (“we”).

1. We do not collect personal data

The App requires no account and does not collect, store or transmit any personally identifiable information. We do not operate any servers that collect data.

2. Photos are processed only on your device

Photos you choose, take or paste are analysed only on your iPhone and are never uploaded to us or any third party. The App uses the system photo picker, so it can only read photos you select. The camera is used only to take a photo for colour picking.

3. Data stored only on your device

Colours you save are stored locally on your iPhone. Deleting the App deletes this data.

4. Email and sharing

When you use “Email Report”, “WhatsApp” or Share, the content is sent by the app you choose (such as Mail or WhatsApp) and is subject to that app’s privacy policy. We never receive it.

5. No ads, no tracking

The App contains no third-party advertising, analytics or tracking, and does not track you across apps or websites. The App has no in-app purchases.

6. Children’s privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal data from anyone, including children.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the “Last updated” date.
